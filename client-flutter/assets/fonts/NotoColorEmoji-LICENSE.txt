iron-link bundles a flags-only SUBSET of the Noto Color Emoji font
(client-flutter/assets/fonts/NotoColorEmoji-Flags.ttf), used so country-flag
emoji in node / subscription names render on Windows (Segoe UI Emoji has no
flag glyphs). The subset keeps only the 26 regional-indicator codepoints
(U+1F1E6..U+1F1FF) and their 259 flag ligatures; no other glyphs.
